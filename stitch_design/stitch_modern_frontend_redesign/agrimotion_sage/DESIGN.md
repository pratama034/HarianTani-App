## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Tactile Organic Modernism** with field-tested mobile ergonomics. Inspired by contemporary intelligent lifestyle and planner mobile interfaces, it balances soft sage atmospheric depth with bold, high-contrast operational touchpoints designed for outdoor agricultural workflows.

The brand persona is dependable, fresh, organized, and deeply rooted in modern agro-technology. It speaks to agricultural producers, agronomists, and farm managers who require precision, clear financial accounting, and effortless single-handed mobile operation under direct sunlight.

Visual hallmarks include:
- Soft, airy sage gradients and misted surface backgrounds (`#EBF3EC` and `#F1F8E9`) providing an organic, glare-reducing canvas.
- Crisp pure white container cards (`#FFFFFF`) with generous curved silhouettes (`24px–28px`).
- Deep forest green anchor actions (`#1B5E20` / `#2E7D32`) paired with energetic fresh leaf accents (`#4CAF50`).
- High-contrast carbon dark accent cards (`#1E2320`) for vital telemetry, key metrics, and completion summaries.
- Pill-shaped touch badges, pill action buttons, and chunky icon containers providing high-confidence tap targets.

## Colors

The color architecture is built for outdoor daylight legibility and immediate financial comprehension:

- **Primary (`#2E7D32` & `#1B5E20`)**: Deep forest greens used for high-importance calls to action, active navigation states, and primary status progress bars.
- **Secondary (`#4CAF50`)**: Fresh leaf green applied to positive accents, chip indicators, active toggles, and AI recommendation badges.
- **Tertiary (`#1B5E20`)**: Deepest botanical green reserved for high-emphasis headlines and elevated actionable iconography.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#EBF3EC`, `#F1F8E9`, `#FFFFFF`)**: A layered tint system. The background canvas uses tinted sage to prevent screen glare in outdoor sunlight, while white cards float above to structure data modules.
- **Dark Accent Card (`#1A221C`)**: Inspired by modern journey cards, these charcoal-forest modules present summaries (e.g., harvest completion, total yield, trip progress) with maximum contrast.
- **Financial Status Semantics**:
  - `status-profit` (`#388E3C`): Agricultural income, completed cycles, positive yields.
  - `status-loss` (`#D32F2F`): Expenses, operational losses, critical agro-alerts.
  - `status-warning` (`#F57C00`): Weather anomalies, delayed irrigation, pest risk warnings.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Space Grotesk** for display headers and numeric readouts with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy, micro-labels, and controls.

- **Space Grotesk** delivers a modern, clean, tech-forward agricultural character. Its open apertures and structural geometry keep operational statistics, crop metrics, and financial totals effortlessly scan-friendly.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings warm, highly-legible humanist qualities that maintain comfort in mobile tables, weather cards, and activity logs.
- All currency readouts adhere to localized Indonesian conventions (`id-ID`, e.g., `Rp 150.000,00`), set in medium-to-bold weights to avoid misinterpretation in outdoor field conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize single-thumb ergonomic navigation on mobile devices with generous touch zones and clean visual padding:

- **Mobile Viewport Target**: 375px to 428px primary width. Outer screen margin is locked at `1.25rem` (20px), ensuring touch safety away from curved smartphone glass edges.
- **Card Padding**: Inner card spacing defaults to `space-md` (16px) for compact widgets and `space-lg` (24px) for prominent hero banners.
- **Interactive Target Standard**: All primary tappable surfaces (buttons, selector chips, quick switches) have a strict minimum height of `48px` to ensure effortless single-handed usage with gloved or wet hands.
- **Module Grid**: Modular card stacks follow a vertical flow separated by `space-md` (16px), allowing independent card groups to be scanned sequentially like an intelligent dashboard.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **Layered Atmospheric Surfaces** rather than deep, murky dropshadows:

- **Ground Canvas**: Subtle sage-tinted backdrop (`#EBF3EC` to `#F1F8E9`) anchors the entire viewport.
- **Surface Level 1 (White Cards)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards float above the sage base with a feather-light organic green-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 60, 35, 0.06)`. This creates crisp contrast without harsh gray boundaries.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Floating Action Buttons & Pill Selectors)**: Primary CTAs utilize a focused lift: `0 10px 20px -2px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.25)`, signaling actionable priority.
- **Surface Level 3 (Dark Summary Pods)**: High-contrast midnight green (`#1A221C`) containers utilize inner illumination rather than drop shadows, with a subtle 1px border stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to ground the card.
- **Borders**: Micro-borders use `#D8E5DA` at 1px thickness on secondary outlines, ensuring structural sharpness under bright ambient light.

## Shapes

The shape signature embraces **Ultra-Rounded Organic Geometry**:

- **Card Containers**: Standardized on a generous `24px` to `28px` corner radius (`rounded-3xl`), mirroring modern travel and journey planner apps.
- **Pill Shapes (Full Capsule / 9999px)**: Applied universally to primary action buttons, filter tags, interactive selector chips, and AI status indicators.
- **Icon Pods**: Circular (`rounded-full`) or super-elliptical (`20px`) backplates with a soft pastel sage fill (`#E8F5E9`), establishing a tactile agricultural tool aesthetic.
- **Nested Inner Modules**: Nested progress tracks, segmented controls, and metric bubbles use `16px` to `20px` radius to harmonize with the exterior container.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Badges
- **Primary Action Button**: Full pill capsule (`height: 52px`), deep forest green (`#2E7D32`), white bold text, subtle lift shadow. Contains contextual left-aligned micro-icons (e.g., sparkles for AI recommendations, `+` for logging).
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Muted sage tint (`#E8F5E9`), forest green label (`#2E7D32`), zero shadow, full capsule corner.
- **Icon Buttons**: Circular white buttons (`44px` to `48px`) with subtle borders and centered charcoal icons, used for header navigation, filters, and bookmarking.

### Cards & Modules
- **White Feature Cards**: Solid `#FFFFFF`, `24px` rounded corners, generous `16px–20px` internal padding, containing activity streams, weather diagnostics, or crop timelines.
- **Dark Telemetry Card**: Deep charcoal-forest `#1A221C` surface with white text, featuring horizontal progress capsules (e.g., "87% Crop Ready"), high-contrast stats, and tactile indicator icons.
- **Agricultural Status Chips**: Pill-shaped badges (`height: 32px`) featuring a leading dot or micro-icon; green background for profit/completion, red for expenses/pests.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields**: Pill or `18px` rounded containers, background filled with soft `#F7FAF7`, 1px border `#D8E5DA`, `50px` height for effortless thumb interaction. Focus state introduces a 2px `#2E7D32` ring.
- **Numeric & Currency Inputs**: Pre-fixed with localized currency symbols (`Rp`), right-aligned bold values in `Space Grotesk`.

### Segmented Timeline & Task Trackers
- Connected vertical timeline routes using dotted and solid forest-green stroke pathways (`#4CAF50` and `#2E7D32`), featuring circular milestone pins for planting, fertilizing, irrigating, and harvest phases.
